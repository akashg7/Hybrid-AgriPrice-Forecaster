🌿 Efficiency-First Backbone

Uses EfficientNet-B0, which employs compound scaling to achieve higher accuracy with 5.3M parameters compared to ResNet-50's 25.6M, making it ideal for agricultural IoT deployment.

⚖️ Data Leakage Protection

Implements Perceptual Hashing instead of just MD5 to find near-duplicate images across merged datasets, ensuring training and test sets are strictly distinct.

⚡ Two-Phase Training

Phase 1: Only trains the classifier head on stable ImageNet features. Phase 2: Unfreezes the top 20 layers for domain-specific fine-tuning on leaf pathology nuances.

🎨 Explainable AI (XAI)

Integrated Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) produces heatmaps showing which parts of the leaf the model attended to—critical for pathology validation.

🔍 Failure Analysis

Automated scripts identify top 10 confusion pairs (e.g., Tomato Late Blight vs. Septoria Leaf Spot) to guide further dataset balancing and multi-label refinement.

💉 Prescriptive Output

The unified dashboard maps categorical disease predictions to actionable fertilizer and pesticide recommendations, bridging the gap between diagnosis and treatment.
