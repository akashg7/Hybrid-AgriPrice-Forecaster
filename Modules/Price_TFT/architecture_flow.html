📊 Multi-Horizon Forecasting

Unlike standard regression, TFT predicts an entire 14-day horizon simultaneously, capturing the complex "decay" of information over future time steps.

🔍 Feature Interpretability (VSN)

The Variable Selection Network identifies which of the input signals (e.g., Rainfall vs. Past Price) are most predictive at each specific time step.

🛡️ Uncertainty Quantification

Uses Quantile Regression to provide risk profiles (P10 to P90), allowing farmers to understand the "worst-case" and "best-case" market scenarios.

🔄 Seasonal Fourier Encodings

Calendar days are mapped to Sin/Cos coordinates, ensuring the model understands that January 1st is chronologically adjacent to December 31st.
