🧠 Architectural Scaling (Heavy-TFT)

The production model features a 128-unit hidden state and a 60-day context window, enabling the system to capture complex annual price cycles and sudden market shocks with 96.15% precision.

🔍 Feature Importance (VSN)

The Variable Selection Network dynamically ranks exogenous signals. During the monsoon, weather covariates (Rainfall, Temp) are automatically weighted higher, while market volume (Arrivals) dominates during harvest gluts.

🛡️ Risk-Aware Quantiles

Instead of single-point forecasts, TFT yields Probabilistic Quantiles (P10, P50, P90). This provides a "Confidence Corridor," helping farmers mitigate risk by seeing the possible downside (P10) of price volatility.

🔄 Fourier Cyclicity

Calendar seasonality is preserved via Circular Fourier Transforms. This allows the model to understand the chronological proximity of December and January, maintaining continuity across fiscal years.
