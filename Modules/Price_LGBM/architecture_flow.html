⚡ Gradient Boosting Core

The system utilizes LightGBM with leaf-wise growth and GOSS (Gradient-based One-Side Sampling) to process 250k+ records with 91.2% accuracy.

🌍 Spatial-Weather Context

By merging IMD/NASA weather data with District Lat/Lon coordinates, the model captures environmental supply shocks (heatwaves, unseasonal rain).

📊 Multi-Horizon Analytics

The Analytics Layer translates raw price trajectories into actionable advice, such as recommend_market.py for profit maximization.
