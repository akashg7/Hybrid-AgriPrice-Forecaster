The model expands 7 raw inputs into **40+ engineered features**, identifying critical biological thresholds like the Aridity Index and Nutrient Dominance.

🏆 Multi-Class Precision

Achieves a **96.4% accuracy** across 22 crop varieties, successfully resolving overlaps between visually similar cereal and pulse ecological niches.

Instead of a single prediction, the engine provides a **Top-5 prioritized list**, allowing farmers to weigh AI confidence against seed availability and market demand.

Designed to consume data directly from Soil Health Card (SHC) schemas, bridging the gap between raw laboratory measurements and field-level planning.
