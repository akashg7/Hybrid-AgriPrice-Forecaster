📂 Multivariate Inputs

Loads dl_30_features_data.csv containing historical prices, arrivals, and 5 weather variables (Temp, Humidity, Rainfall, Solar, Wind) mapped to daily records.

🧩 Variable Selection

TFT uses specialized Variable Selection Networks to automatically weigh the importance of weather vs. historical lags for each forecast specific to a Mandy/Commodity pair.

🧠 Self-Attention Layer

Processes the 30-day lookback window using Multi-Head Attention to identify long-range patterns (e.g., month-long price cycles) that standard RNNs or gradient boosters may miss.

📊 Probabilistic Outputs

Uses Quantile Loss to predict the 10th, 50th (median), and 90th percentiles, providing farmers with price "confidence bands" rather than just a single point prediction.

🛡️ System Stability

Implements Gradient Clipping (0.1) and Early Stopping during training on the Lightning framework to ensure the deep transformer converges reliably on volatile Indian market data.

🔗 Integration

Forecasts are pre-generated and stored in Parquet format, allowing the serve.py backend to provide instant, reactive multi-horizon charts to end users.
